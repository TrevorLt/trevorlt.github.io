CryptScan

A small step into 3D scanning.

A course project exploring how a distance sensor, a rotating platform and a Raspberry Pi Pico 2 can turn measurements into a point cloud.

Focus
Physical computing
Stage
In development
Platform
Raspberry Pi Pico 2
Inside the project

The planned setup combines a distance sensor with a rotating platform. Height is adjusted by hand, with measurements intended to become a simple 3D point cloud.

I’m developing this project through DSGN 297. My related Pico 2 coursework includes connecting motors, a distance sensor and NeoPixels, along with soldering components and wiring.

The current focus is getting a coarse scanning setup working. A complete scan has not yet been demonstrated.

About me

Robotics is what I’m most excited to explore.

I’m Trevor, a computer engineering student at Northwestern University with a passion for robotics. I’m exploring how sensing, mechanisms and software come together to help a machine understand and interact with the world.

I learn best by building and experimenting. CryptScan is one way I’m exploring that interest, starting with distance measurements and a simple rotating platform. Alongside hardware, I enjoy refining useful software and learning what makes a small tool feel clear and dependable.
