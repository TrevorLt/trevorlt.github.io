CryptScan

A small step into 3D scanning.

A course project exploring how a distance sensor, a rotating platform and a Raspberry Pi Pico 2 can turn measurements into a point cloud.

Focus
Physical computing
Stage
In development
Platform
Raspberry Pi Pico 2
Inside the project

The planned setup combines a distance sensor with a rotating platform. Height is adjusted by hand, with measurements intended to become a simple 3D point cloud.

I’m developing this project through DSGN 297. My related Pico 2 coursework includes connecting motors, a distance sensor and NeoPixels, along with soldering components and wiring.

The current focus is getting a coarse scanning setup working. A complete scan has not yet been demonstrated.

About me

I like understanding a system by making something with it.

I’m Trevor, a computer engineering student at Northwestern University. I’m interested in the space where physical systems meet software: how a sensor becomes a measurement, how a mechanism moves, and how an interface makes a tool easier to use.

My projects take me between prototyping and the screen. Sometimes that means wiring and soldering around a Pico 2. Other times, it means refining a small Mac app with AI-assisted development. I value a working, understandable result and a clear account of what I contributed.
